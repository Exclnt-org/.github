# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Exclnt is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in our community and projects.

We expect all community members to contribute in a way that supports learning, collaboration, and mutual respect.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate.
* Communicate constructively.
* Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
* Give and receive constructive feedback.
* Focus discussions on ideas, projects, and solutions.
* Respect the boundaries and privacy of other community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory or hateful conduct.
* Unwanted sexual or inappropriate behavior.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Deliberate disruption of community discussions or projects.
* Trolling or other behavior intended to create a hostile environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within Exclnt spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories
* Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Community channels
* Other official Exclnt spaces

Project-specific rules may provide additional requirements.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the Exclnt maintainers through the appropriate private communication channel.

Please provide enough information to help the maintainers understand the situation and take appropriate action.

Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Possible actions may include:

* A warning
* Removal of inappropriate content
* Temporary restriction from community spaces
* Removal from a project or community space

The response will depend on the circumstances and severity of the violation.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is based on common open-source community guidelines and has been adapted for the Exclnt community.
